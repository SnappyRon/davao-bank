Bantay asks for extra confirmation when a payment looks unusual, for example a new payee,
a larger amount than usual, several quick payments, a new device, or a late hour. No money
is sent until you confirm with your password and authenticator code. If you do not
recognise the payment, lock your account in Bantay.
